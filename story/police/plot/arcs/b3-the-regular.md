# The Regular

## Setup
Days 1 to 5. A bath house man sits at the tea counter every morning and never pays. The clerk pours, waves off the queue, and tucks a paper tab under the till. Officer A drops in at the pools in a civilian jacket and takes a rolled towel. The front desk attendant looks at the floor when the regular walks by. Nothing is announced; the camera just keeps seeing the same small things on different days.

## Rising Action
Days 6 to 11. A leaves with a towel roll past the veteran's bench. The regular rides to a suite on nine at the Cloud Pagoda. The clerk walks out on d07 and does not come back. A's patrol skips the bath house door, and B says out loud that they turned left yesterday. A lifts a folded page from the till drawer. A paper slip from a lift mugger names the tea counter and locker 14. The front desk attendant vanishes on d09. A poster of the clerk is painted over on the station screens. A comm voice that sounds like B talks to the courier at the suite door. A lurker is caught on the platform. Two red herrings run beside this: a bath regular who pays cash in odd amounts, and a night courier at a tenements door at 03:00.

## Climax
Days 12 to 15. Locker 14 is opened and the courier is arrested (d12). A ledger page marked with B's initials is logged, the evidence bag goes missing, and G posts B's suspension (d13). The suite on nine is searched and burned (d13). B's notebook, the call log and the towel roll in A's locker clear B on d14. On d15 A walks into the bath house in civilian clothes and tells the regular by comm to stay out of the pools; the stakeout finds an empty stool.

## Resolution
Days 17 to 18 (CARELESS, no cooperation; CAREFUL moves to d19 to d21; CARELESS with station 2 cooperating moves to d13 to d15, see deltas). The attendant is found on d17 and talks. On d18 at 07:40 the regular sits on his stool for the same tea; C and B arrest him and A walks in to warn him and is arrested too. The tab, the credits and the towels all close on one counter.

## Cast and hidden truth (story team only)
- The regular is the boss (boss-careless in the base ledger; boss-careful in the CAREFUL rows). Everyone at the bath house treats him as furniture. He never pays because the tea counter credits are bought by strangers with cash and then drawn down by him: laundering through tea credits. He also trades on pool gossip (who is leaving, which officer is on which route).
- g2 clerk (go-between-clerk) keeps the paper tabs and passes exact change. She slips away on d07 when the veteran starts sitting on the bench. Her poster is covered on d10 (s6) and restored on d14.
- g1 courier (go-between-courier) carries bags to locker 14 and to the Cloud Pagoda suite on nine. Arrested d12.
- g3 (go-between-bartender, CAREFUL only) works the pool cart, passes tokens at the lift, and carries what g1 cannot after d12.
- A (inside-officer) takes towel rolls (credit slips inside), skips the door on scans, lifts the till page, marks it with B's initials, makes a comm call in B's rough voice to the courier, steals the evidence bag, and tips the regular on d15.
- B (framed-officer) is stubborn and by the book, writes everything in a notebook, and notices the route change.
- Informant: bath house front desk attendant (informant). Sees the towel hand-overs. Vanishes d09 (s7), found d17, talks.
- Safe house: Cloud Pagoda suite 9F. CAREFUL second safe house: tenements flat 3B.
- Red herrings: rh2 (bath regular, cash in odd amounts, gambling debt and a cousin loan; the stranger on the platform is the cousin) and rh1 (night courier, visiting a partner at tenements door 4C).
