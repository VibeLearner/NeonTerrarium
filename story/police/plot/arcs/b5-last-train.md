# The Last Train

## Setup
The metro platform attendant keeps the lost property bin and a paper cup ready for A every morning. Umbrellas and scarves with colored tags go into the bin; other commuters claim them; a courier carries paper bags from bench 3 to a flat in the tenements. A swaps onto the metro patrol and B starts noticing small things. The clerk at the tag drawer watches the attendant and the floor.

## Rising Action
Tagged items are handed hand to hand (d03 to d08). A's roster swap and skipped scans are logged (d05, d07). A mugger's slip, a lifted envelope and a platform lurker all point at the bin and the 07:12 train (d07 to d11). The clerk vanishes (d09). B's scan log shows a scan of the courier he never made (d10). The tenements flat is opened and found empty (d13). A's call at the wrong hour, an empty evidence cabinet and B's suspension follow (d14, d15).

## Climax
d17 the veteran finds the clerk on the lawn; the rookie and the sergeant read the edit stamp that clears B. d18 the last train: a stakeout on the platform, the attendant turns the bin key at 06:40, and B arrests A on the platform while the veteran hands the beat to the rookie.

## Resolution
The attendant is arrested, A is arrested by his own partner, B is cleared and the post is corrected. The veteran's last shift ends; the rookie takes the loop. CARELESS with cooperating stations ends d14 to d15. CAREFUL ends d20 after a second safe house (a foundry room) and a platform sweeper are peeled away.

## Cast and hidden truth
Hidden truth (story team only): the metro platform attendant is the crime boss. Payoffs for the Square's weekly tip are left in the lost property bin inside tagged items, claimed by a chain of ordinary commuters, and carried by the courier (g1) from bench 3 to a tenements flat (5D). A (inside-officer) requested the metro patrol swap, skips the bin on scans, and takes calls at the wrong hour ("Not here."). He alters B's scan log at front desk terminal FD-2 at 22:15 to show a scan of the courier. The lost property clerk (informant) has seen the attendant take the bin tags for months; he vanishes d09 (s7) and is found on the lawn d17, where he talks. CAREFUL adds a platform sweeper (g3, go-between-bartender id) who carries the take to a foundry room g1 never visits, and an extra insider (inside-officer-extra) at station 2. Red herrings: the night courier (rh1) visits a partner at tenements 3A; the bath regular (rh2) repays a cousin.

Who token note: the cover face is logged as `boss-careless` in BASE and CARELESS rows and `boss-careful` in CAREFUL rows. Camera text never uses the word boss. The cover face is called "attendant" in camera text.
