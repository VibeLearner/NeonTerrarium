# Veteran Officer (flag C)

## Role
Swing shift, pair 2 with rookie-officer. A few weeks from retirement. Slow, deliberate, notices things nobody mentions.

## Look and tells (camera-safe)
- Stands longer than needed at corners (stand, then a small point at something).
- Counts the queue at the Dome door with his lips moving.
- Asks small questions twice, a day apart, and listens to see if the answer changed (chat).
- Eats the same meal at the same food-counter and watches who else is regular there.
- Turns down the stairs, takes the lift, sits in the lift hall (sit) and watches the lobby.

## Thread
Notices the pattern first and says almost nothing. Quietly keeps the informant talking (informant is his confidant). Retires on the last day or is the one who breaks the case on his last shift. Never spoon-feeds the rookie.

## Pair 2 dynamic (ladder P2-1 to P2-5)
Teacher and pupil, rookie gets impatient, rookie pulls ahead, veteran retires, torch passed (or broken if rookie acts too early).

## Flags
C:retired, C:suspended, C:retirement-delayed
