# Dead Drop Days

## Setup
The logistics hub dispatcher sits at terminal T-07 and sends bots and drones out in numbered order. One tray a day leaves with no recipient line. Parcels land on tenement doorsteps; a runner with no delivery list knocks at the same doors. A, the friendly officer, patrols past the hub window and never scans its door. B writes everything in a paper notebook. C eats lunch on the bench by the bot bay, next to the mechanic who says nothing.

## Rising Action
d01 to d08: heavy parcels are thumbed at the base, swapped at doorsteps and sent from the hub under a gold-edged T-07 sticker. A's route bends left past the hub window and a flat parcel changes hands through it (d07). A comm call in B's voice tells a resident to hold a parcel by the door (d08). d09 E finds the missing parcel with a hollow base and a label under terminal T-07. d10 a wanted poster of the doorstep courier is sprayed over on the station screens. d11 D finds six blank mornings in the hub scan log while G posts a delivery log with B's badge on it; B is framed. d12 the mechanic leaves the bot bay and does not return. d13 H's dawn watch at the bay door is spoiled when A walks in with takeaway; B is suspended on the screens and the false-bottom parcel is gone from the evidence shelf.

## Climax
d14 H and D wait in the tenement hall and arrest the courier at a doorstep; the sprayed poster is restored and the neighbor's receipt clears the rent argument. d15 the hydroponic farm shed is opened (crates, panels, a comm rig) and G matches the rig's call times to B's own screen camera, clearing him. d16 C finds the mechanic on the lawn and the statement is overheard. d17 the last stakeout: the dispatcher hands A a flat parcel at the window and B arrests his own partner while C and H take the dispatcher.

## Resolution
The dispatcher and A are arrested, B is cleared and back at his desk, the notice is corrected. C hands D the beat sheet. CARELESS with cooperating stations ends d15 (closed d15). CAREFUL adds a drone bay fixer (g3), a second flat in the tenements and an extra insider at station 2; the dispatcher slips on d17 to a flat, and the arrest comes d20, closed d21.

## Cast and hidden truth
Hidden truth (story team only): the logistics hub dispatcher is the crime boss. The weekly payoffs ride in false-bottom parcels under terminal T-07. Bots and drones carry them to doorsteps; the courier (g1) swaps or collects them at doors that are on no delivery list; A takes a flat parcel through the hub window and keeps the hub's door off every scan. The hydroponic farm shed is the safe house: parcels are opened there and a comm rig there makes calls in B's voice. The delivery log under B's badge (2207) is entered from the dispatcher's terminal. The bot bay mechanic fits and repairs the false panels, saw all of it, and slips away on d12 after B is framed. Red herrings: the night courier at 5B (a partner, hidden from a landlord) and the stall neighbor (rent, and a bag of cloth stock).

Who token note: the cover face is logged as `boss-careless` in BASE and CARELESS rows and `boss-careful` in CAREFUL rows. Camera text never uses the word boss. The cover face is called "dispatcher" in camera text. "T-07" is terminal T-07. Camera text calls g1 "courier", g3 "fixer" and the informant "mechanic".
