# Go-Between Clerk (g2)

A counter job: tea counter, snack stand, lost property, a shopfront till. Keeps the ledger of who owes what and passes it on.

## Camera-safe tells
- Marks a paper tab at the counter and tucks it under the till, not the ledger.
- Chats about the weather with the face every day (chat), then hands over change that is too exact (hand-over).
- Leaves early on Fridays (leave).
- When pushed, disappears for two days and returns with a different haircut or coat.

## Thread
Slips away mid-case in some boss cases (g2:missing). Returns or is found. Flags: g2:missing, g2:arrested, g2:talking.
