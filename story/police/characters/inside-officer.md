# Inside Officer (flag A)

## Role
Day shift, pair 1 with framed-officer. Works for the crime boss. Passes patrol schedules, delays scans, steers evidence, helps frame his own partner.

## Look and tells (camera-safe)
- Friendly. Offers rides, buys rounds, remembers everyone's order.
- On patrol: police-patrol routes drift past the boss's cover face. Skips the face's door on police-scan.
- Eats at the boss's counter, hand-over of a tip never seen, only a nod.
- Takes a call at the wrong hour and steps outside to take it (phone: one side, short answers, "Not here.").
- Swaps shifts with desk-sergeant before big stakeouts.

## Thread
Helpful leak, then the squeeze: schedules leak, evidence thins, B is framed. In CAREFUL cases an extra inside officer (inside-officer-extra) covers each extra station. Ends arrested, or slips if the case is set back.

## Pair 1 dynamic (ladder P1-1 to P1-5)
Starts easy and warm. Lies get small, then routes change, then B is suspended. Ends cold: B cleared and A arrested, or B fired and A promoted by the leak's cover.

## Flags
A:suspended, A:arrested, A:reassigned, A:promoted
