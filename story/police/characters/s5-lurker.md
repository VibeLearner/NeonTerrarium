# S5 Lurker
A young man, early twenties, paid in tea credits. Never does anything that announces why he is there.
## Look and camera-safe tells
- Gray wool coat, orange scarf, hood down, pencil behind the ear.
- sit on the platform bench between trains; stand at the yellow line when a train stops; never enter a train.
- wave: raises one hand toward the attendant booth as each train stops.
- Writes in pencil on a scrap, drops it under the bench slat.
- Sits at the corner table at bath-house/tea-counter without ordering; pays with a leaf-stamped chip.
- Gives up without running when H steps off the pillar.
