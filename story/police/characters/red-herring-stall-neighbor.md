# Red Herring: Stall Neighbor (rh3)

Innocent resident, runs the next stall or shopfront. Argues loudly with the go-between courier about rent, and carries a heavy bag at night. It is rent owed to the shop landlord, and a bag of stock he is hiding from a competitor.

## Camera-safe tells
- argue with the courier at the stall line.
- carry a heavy bag at 23:00 along the Plaza.
- Stands outside the station front screens for ten minutes (stand).
- Cleared when the landlord's receipt is read (document) and the bag is shown to be stock.
