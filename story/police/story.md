# Neon Terrarium Police

## Synopsis

A surveillance camera follows the officers of a floating neon city while a crime boss hides behind an ordinary face. Storylets, not chapters.

## Tone & Style

Camera-log voice. Only what a surveillance monitor shows: actions, overheard speech, one side of a comm call, mesh posts, documents on screen. Never thoughts or conclusions. Cozy and wet: rain, umbrellas, steam, neon.

## Notes

Storyboard bible for a storylet database: chapters are game days (chapter-01 to chapter-21). Start at notes/master-design.md. Run `python3 story/check_bible.py` for the fair-play checker, then the story CLI maintenance block.
