# Stall Fire

## Setup
A stall at market-plaza burns at 22:41 on d05. F gets the case because nobody else will take it. He eats at odd hours and skips scans.

## Rising Action
A melted gel cap, a fire log that says the supply was fine, and the owner says a man came Thursday and said the usual. F sits too long, argues about a shift swap, and nearly drops the case.

## Climax
d09: F waits at the plaza seats, the suspect comes back to the burnt stall, F arrests him and finds a gel vial and the other half of a scorched stub in his pocket.

## Resolution
d10: the statement at the station. The stall owner opens under a new awning on d13 and F walks the corners again.

## Cast and hidden truth
The suspect (s3-suspect) is a young man paid twenty credits by the courier (g1) to burn a stall that refused a weekly payment. He is also cousin to the Dome snack stand worker (the informant in b2), who told him the stall had refused. The rival vendor and the melted power strip are red herrings. The note under the counter is the demand.

Minor characters: s3-stall-owner, s3-suspect, s3-rival-vendor.
