# Burnout Officer (flag F)

## Role
Swing shift. Burning out. Runs the stall fire case. The case is the thing that makes him sit up, or breaks him.

## Look and tells (camera-safe)
- Skips police-scan at corners, sits too long on a seat in the Town Square (sit, eat, sit).
- Eats at odd hours alone (eat at the food-counter at 15:00 and 22:30).
- Reads the same notice three times (read-notice), shrugs at the sergeant (shrug).
- Argues about shift swaps (argue), asks for leave.

## Thread
Gets the fire case because nobody else wants it. A real clue wakes him. He nearly drops it, then makes the arrest (or goes on leave with the case cold).
