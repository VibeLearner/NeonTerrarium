# Lift Lobby Muggings

## Setup
Past 03:00 someone grabs takeaway bags at the lift hall. H takes the night door; D keeps notes and itches to chase. Five victims in ten nights, nothing else taken.

## Rising Action
H stands the same corner three nights. D reads the lobby log aloud, chases a night courier who is only visiting a partner, and finds a torn paper scrap in the lift floor gap.

## Climax
d08: the grab happens in front of H. The man in the green scarf runs for the stairs and is arrested. A folded route slip is in his left sleeve.

## Resolution
The slip goes to the desk sergeant and into the case file. The lobby guard is cleared. D returns a recovered bag. The lift hall goes quiet.

## Cast and hidden truth
Mugger (s1-mugger) is a broke runner who carries bowls and paper bags for the courier (g1) for ten credits a run. The grabs were his own side money, only ever takeaway bags from the food-counter. He never met anyone above the courier. The slip is the courier's weekly route sheet.

Minor characters: s1-mugger, s1-victim, s1-victim-b, s1-lobby-guard.
