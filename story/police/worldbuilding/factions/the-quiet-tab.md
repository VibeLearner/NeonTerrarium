# The Quiet Tab

## Purpose

A crime ring that takes a small weekly "tab" from stalls, doors and counters and fences what falls off the delivery lines. Small, regular, never loud.

## Power Base

One cover face per case, one or two go-betweens, safe houses, and an inside officer. In CAREFUL cases an extra layer and an inside officer per extra station.

## Members

boss (two variants), go-between-courier, go-between-clerk, go-between-bartender, inside-officer, inside-officer-extra.

## Conflicts

Internal and external pressures.
