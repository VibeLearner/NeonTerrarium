# Platform Lurker

## Setup
From d04 a young man in a gray coat and orange scarf sits on the metro platform bench every night and never boards. H, the night officer who stands and watches doors, takes the post at the pillar.

## Rising Action
H sees a raised hand at every train, a scrap of pencil times, and a paper cup passed from the lost property bin ledge. On d09 the same coat sits across from the tea counter at the bath house. An old man on the bench and a woman in a gray coat look like the same watcher and are not.

## Climax
d11, 23:22: H steps off the pillar and arrests the lurker at the yellow line. The pockets hold the pencil scrap and a leaf-stamped tea chip.

## Resolution
d13 the gray coat is cleared behind the counter. d14-d15 H bags the scrap and G logs it against the attendant's shift sheet. The platform bench stays empty.

## Cast and hidden truth
The lurker is a paid lookout: he reports train arrival times to the platform attendant (b5 cover face) by hand signal and a word at the hatch after the last train, and he sits across from the bath house tea counter as a lookout for the regular (b3 cover face). He is paid in tea, in cups at the bin ledge and in leaf chips. He is not violent and names no one. The attendant and the regular are never named by this case; the boss arcs supply the local meaning.
