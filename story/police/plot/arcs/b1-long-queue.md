# The Long Queue

Camera note: in camera text the officers appear by flag letter (A inside officer, B framed officer, C veteran, D rookie, E family caller, F burnout, G desk sergeant, H night stakeout). Other roles appear as keeper (noodle stall), courier, neighbor, restocker (informant), resident.

## Setup
The noodle stall in the Town Square food stalls has the longest queue in the city, every day, same hour. The keeper ladles, nods, and tucks folded slips into a lid rack. A, the friendly day shift officer, eats there and never pays. B, his partner, pays and writes it down. C, a few weeks from retirement, sits on the bench by the vending machines where the restocker eats lunch without speaking. Default roll: CARELESS (one go-between, one safe house).

## Rising Action
Days 1 to 7: small repeated oddities. Folded slips under bowls, a courier who carries bowls nobody eats, door 4C, A skipping the stall on scans and lengthening his route past it. Red herrings run beside them: a stall neighbor shouting at the courier, a night courier at door 6B at 03:00. Days 8 to 11: three small cases cross. A mugger carries a route slip, a parcel with a false bottom carries a lid number, an arsonist was paid by the courier, and the restocker vanishes. Then the frame arrives: a payoff label and a feed post carrying B's badge number.

## Climax
Days 12 to 15: B is posted as suspended on the station screens, the evidence tray goes empty, H stakes out the lift hall and sees A hand a folded sheet to the courier. The courier is arrested. A warns the keeper before dawn, the shutter drops, and flat 4C is cleaned out before C and D open it.

## Resolution
Day 17 the restocker is found on the lawn and talks. Day 18 G reads the terminal log, corrects the post, and B is cleared. Day 19 the queue forms again at 11:50 and H, who has watched the stall for three days, makes the arrest; A is arrested with the missing tray in his locker. Day 20 the stall is taped and the case closes.

## Cast and hidden truth
- The keeper (boss-careless) runs the "Square tip": every stall owner pays a weekly sum, folded into a slip under an empty bowl, tucked in the lid rack. Lids are stenciled 11 to 17, one per stall.
- Courier (g1) carries lidded bowls, empty of food and full of cash, from the stall to flat 4C in the tenements (the one safe house). The resident of 4C counts and returns a thin paper bag.
- A takes free bowls and a cut; he skips the stall on scans, lengthens his route past it, passes patrol sheets to the courier, and takes a call he will not finish ("Not here.").
- Frame: A writes B's badge number on a payoff label with a looped 7, posts from a station terminal at 14:02 while B stands lift lobby duty, and lets G's paper-first habit do the rest. A empties the evidence tray.
- Informant (restocker) sees the stall every day, steps back after the mugger is arrested, vanishes d09, is found d17.
- rh3 (stall neighbor) owes rent to the landlord and hides stock from a competitor. rh1 (night courier) visits a partner at 6B who hides him from the landlord.
