# OW Umbrella Seller
Sells umbrellas under the market-plaza awning on rainy days.
## Look and camera-safe tells
- Yellow raincoat, rack of folded umbrellas, paper tags with numbers on each handle.
- hand-over of an umbrella to anyone who points at the rack.
