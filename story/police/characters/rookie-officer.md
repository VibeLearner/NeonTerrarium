# Rookie Officer (flag D)

## Role
Swing shift, pair 2. Eager, keeps notes, over-reads rules. Runs small cases (pickpocket, muggings). Wants to chase.

## Look and tells (camera-safe)
- Runs when the veteran would walk.
- Reads notices out loud, points at things, asks too many questions in the lift hall.
- Writes in a notebook while walking; bumps into people.
- Hugs a relative on the lawn after a bad shift (hug), then straightens the uniform.

## Thread
Chases the wrong resident (a red herring), is held back, gets the right call late. Ends promoted or still the student depending on the case.

## Pair 2 dynamic
Starts as pupil, grows impatient, pulls ahead, inherits the veteran's beat.

## Flags
D:promoted, D:reprimanded, D:injured
