# Ordinary Police Work

## Setup
The background of every run: three shifts, eight officers, patrols and scans, thieves, lurkers, rain and umbrellas. Beats repeat on a loop by shift (day, swing, night). A beat can fire in any case; the ledger gives the first firing and the trigger in the title.

## Rising Action
Each loop leaves small paper and small habits: a rota, a scan slate, a receipt, a glove, a tag. Boss and small cases borrow beats and read the same paper.

## Climax
There is no peak. The loops show who keeps the rhythm (G, C) and who skips it (F, E on a call).

## Resolution
d17 to d21: the thieves are caught, F scans corner 3, the rota moves and the small papers are handed in.

## Cast and hidden truth
Nothing hidden. Real clues are habits and small paper that other cases lean on. Two red herrings: the hooded person at the shutter (ow-c05) and F with a taped bag (ow-c09). Repeat rule: after the first firing in the ledger the beat may fire again on the same trigger every 3 to 5 days with different faces; the clue tokens belong only to the first firing.
