# Boss, CARELESS variant

The crime boss when the roll is CARELESS. Exists once the city has the Neon Dome or the Market Plaza. Hides behind an ordinary face the player may already watch. The cover face differs per case: noodle stall keeper (b1), Dome door worker (b2), bath house regular (b3), logistics hub dispatcher (b4), metro platform attendant (b5).

## Structure
Layers: boss, g1 (go-between-courier), sometimes g2 (go-between-clerk). One safe house. One inside officer (inside-officer). No extra insiders. Payoffs passed hand to hand, small and regular.

## Camera-safe tells
- Same routine every day; the face is where the face always is.
- Pays in small hand-overs; never carries a bag.
- Greets A first and by nickname; never greets B.
- Does not move the safe house when a go-between is arrested (boss:safehouse-burned follows within two days).
- When several stations cooperate (station2:cooperating), the routine is too regular to survive: arrest around d13 to d15.

## Thread
Easy money, easy habits, caught sooner when the stations talk to each other.
