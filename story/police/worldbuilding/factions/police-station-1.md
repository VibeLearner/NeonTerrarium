# Police Station 1

## Purpose

Keeps the streets calm. 8 officers on three shifts: day, swing, night. About one station per 600 residents.

## Power Base

The station front screens (wanted posters), the evidence cage, briefing, patrol routes, scan handhelds.

## Members

Pair 1: inside-officer and framed-officer (day). Pair 2: veteran-officer and rookie-officer (swing). Also family-caller-officer, burnout-officer, desk-sergeant, night-stakeout-officer.

## Conflicts

Internal and external pressures.
