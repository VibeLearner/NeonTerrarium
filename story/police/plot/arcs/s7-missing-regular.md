# Missing Regular

## Setup
The informant eats at bench 3 at the food-counter, silent beside C. On d08 they slide a folded paper under the slat and leave their meal. On d09 they do not come back.

## Rising Action
C counts the bench twice, knocks at 6B, and hears a bag went to the lift at 23:40. The flat is tidy. A shift lead leaves a vest bag, a visitor knocks at 03:05, and a neighbor carries lunch boxes toward the lawn.

## Climax
d17: C walks onto the lawn, sits on the cloth by the sister, and eats beside the informant without a word.

## Resolution
d18-d19 the informant sits at C's desk and talks for forty minutes and hands over a bag of folded slips.

## Cast and hidden truth
The informant left by their own choice on the night of d08 carrying a bag and has stayed with their sister on the lawn, fed by a neighbor. The informant's workplace and what they saw come from the boss case: the vending machine restocker (b1), the bath house front desk attendant (b3), the lost property clerk (b5). They will talk only to C. Nobody took them.
