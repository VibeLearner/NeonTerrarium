# Sky Mall Pickpocket

## Setup
A kiosk clerk counts paper stubs on a bench at the sky-mall at 16:00. D picks up one she drops and gives it back. The next day her envelope is gone.

## Rising Action
A gray hood passes the bench at 16:06. D chases a teenager and a colleague with a similar envelope and is held back by C. A badge number and a bin of stubs follow.

## Climax
d12: D stands at the tea kiosk without running. The gray hood sits on the bench at 16:00 and D arrests him with a stub in his pocket.

## Resolution
The stubs go back to the owner on d13. D hugs a relative on the lawn on d14 and reads his notebook.

## Cast and hidden truth
The thief (s4-thief) is a mall maintenance worker, paid forty credits by a stranger to lift whatever the clerk left on the bench. The envelope held perforated stubs (Dome snack tokens and tabs in b2, lost property tags with a platform number in b5), worthless to him: he binned most of them. The teenager and the colleague are red herrings. Clerk is the hand that is meant to pass the stubs on.

Minor characters: s4-owner, s4-thief, s4-teen, s4-colleague.
