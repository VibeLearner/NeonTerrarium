# Screen Graffiti

## Setup
From d08 orange paint appears on the wanted posters on police-station/front-screens, always on poster 4. G runs the screens and sees the paper first.

## Rising Action
Three dots, then a smear, then on d10 poster 4 is covered edge to edge and G posts 'screen 4 offline'. G finds small sneaker prints, a spray can with a blue bot sticker, and a lanyard turned inward. A cleaner with a cloth and a dripping drone cloud the picture.

## Climax
d14: G posts the reprint of poster 4. The teen with the lanyard stands at the glass and talks to G for two minutes.

## Resolution
d15-d16 H watches the hall at night and nothing is painted. d17 the teen signs a sheet and cleans the glass with a broom and bucket.

## Cast and hidden truth
The painter is a teenager, younger sibling of the face on poster 4. The face is a go-between: the missing clerk g2 in b3, the courier g1 in b4. The teen covers the face to keep it off the screens and does not paint any other poster. Nobody pays the teen and no gang is involved.
